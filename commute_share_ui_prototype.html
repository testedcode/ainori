UI design system

Modern, trustworthy and commuter-first.

The style direction uses crisp cards, deep blue trust colors, green savings cues, large rounded controls, visible route cards and high-contrast CTAs.

Product principles used in this UI

1
Destination-first booking

The user starts with route and arrival time; the app shows all matching rides immediately.

2
Trust before transaction

Driver identity, workplace verification, ratings, seat rules and cancellation terms appear before payment.

3
Professional commute habits

Recurring routes, office clusters, quiet ride preference, punctuality and company communities are built in.

4
Equal rider and sharer flows

The driver flow is as easy as booking: route, car, price, preview, publish.
